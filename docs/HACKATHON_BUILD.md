# How this was built

- Event: LA Hacks AI Hackathon, UCLA, Oct 17–18, 2026 (hacking started Oct 17 12:00 PDT).
- Team of three. Idea and research synthesis before the event; all code in this repository was written during the
  event.
- An earlier same-day prototype (a GlowSlim tea ad checker with ASI:One cards, by Person 3) proved the ASI:One card
  flow and the uAgents mailbox setup. Its patterns (card builders, click parsing, offline chat harness) informed this
  build; its product scope was replaced by the AeroBeat electronics scope in the team's master brief.
- AI tools: Claude (architecture and implementation of this build) and Devin (as planned in the team brief). Commit
  history is the record of what was added when.
